NY Tax Law § 253 & NYC Admin. Code § 11-2601

Mortgage recording tax in New York City includes both the NYS basic tax and local NYC surcharges. For 1-to-3 family residential dwellings, mortgages under $500,000 are taxed at 2.05%, while larger loans trigger the 2.175% rate.
