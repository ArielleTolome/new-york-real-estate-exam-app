Under New York Real Property Law, how is the statutory mortgage recording tax calculated on a residential purchase financed with an institutional lender in New York City?

A

A flat state transfer surcharge of $500 plus 0.5% of the total loan commitment.

B

2.05% of the mortgage amount on loans under $500,000, or 2.175% for loans of $500,000 or greater.

Correct answer
C

1.0% paid exclusively by the mortgagor at initial commitment.

D

Exempt from county and city recording duties if under $750,000.

Your answer
Incorrect. The correct answer is B.
Statutory analysis NY RPL § 253

Legal Citation & Rationale

NY Tax Law § 253 & NYC Admin Code § 11-2601

Mortgage recording tax within New York City includes the New York State basic tax, special additional tax, and local NYC recording surcharges.

For 1-to-3 family residential dwellings, loan obligations under $500,000 incur a total rate of 2.05% (with the lender paying 0.25% of that statutory requirement).

Once the mortgage indebtedness reaches or exceeds $500,000, the applicable rate steps up to 2.175%. New York provides no blanket exemption for institutional mortgages below $750,000.
