1

OLD CAR fiduciary framework

NYS standard

An agent owes obedience, loyalty, disclosure, confidentiality, accountability, and reasonable care to their client. Confidentiality survives contract closing indefinitely.

2

Creation of agency relationship

RPL § 443

Agency is determined by mutual consent and fiduciary delegation, never by who pays the brokerage commission fee.

3

Dual agency with designated agents

DOS form 1754-a

Permissible only with written informed consent of both principals. Inherent conflict neutralizes individual loyalty unless designated sales agents are appointed by the supervising broker.

4

Statutory disclosure form presentation

Reading now

Under NY Real Property Law § 443, licensees must provide the New York State Disclosure Form at the first substantive contact prior to discussing financial qualifications or confidential property motivations.

NY RPL § 443(3)(a) Exam test frequency: Very high
5

Refusal to sign disclosure declaration

19 NYCRR § 175.7

If a prospective buyer or seller refuses to sign the statutory acknowledgment, the licensee must prepare an affidavit detailing the date, time, and circumstances of refusal, retaining it for 3 years.

6

Brokerage vicarious liability

RPL § 442-c

A broker is vicariously liable for unlawful conduct of affiliated salespersons only if the broker had actual knowledge or retained illegal profits after notice.
